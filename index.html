05 / RESEARCH NOTEBOOK

The point of the record is not to hide critique. It is to show what changed because of it.

RESEARCH / 01PREPRINT

AI-Assisted Healthcare Systems: Bridging the Implementation Gap Through a Human-Centered Perspective

An independent review of 18 peer-reviewed studies across clinical support, documentation, patient engagement, accessibility and governance, focused on human oversight and implementation barriers.

QUESTION→REVIEW→SUBMISSION→EDITORIAL FEEDBACK→METHODOLOGICAL LESSON→NEXT ITERATION
Research integrity note

The manuscript was submitted to JMIR AI and was not accepted for journal publication. Editorial feedback praised conceptual strengths while identifying a small evidence base for the breadth of the review, single-reviewer bias, missing search-date parameters and insufficient quality appraisal. The next version needs a more rigorous protocol, broader evidence and independent review.

11 / RESEARCH PRACTICE

Open science as a working habit

NASA Open Science Essentials introduced me to FAIR principles, repositories, licensing, version control, persistent identifiers and the idea that research becomes more useful when other people can inspect, reproduce and build on it. I am still learning to apply those practices consistently.

Good systems should not depend on one person understanding everything. Good research should not either.
